Using Cookies In order for the website www.par-conference.com to work properly and to improve it with the aim of improving your user’s experience, it is necessary to store the minimum amount of information (cookies) on your computer or a mobile device. By using this website, you agree to the use of cookies by whose blocking you can still browse the website, but some of its features will not be functioning. To avoid using cookies, it is necessary to set your computer settings to delete cookies from websites and / or to notify you about stored cookies on your computer. What Kind of Cookies Do We Use? Session cookies – these are temporary cookies which expire (and are automatically deleted) when you close your internet browser. We use these cookies to allow access to content and enable commenting (things you must do when logging on using your information). Persistent cookies – usually they have an expiry date far into the future thus they will remain in your browser until they expire, or until you delete them manually. We use persistent cookies to better understand users’ habits so we can improve the webpage according to your habits. This information is anonymous – we cannot see individual user’s information. Does the Website Contain Third-party Cookies? There are several external services that save limited cookies. These cookies are not set up by this site, but some of them are used for the normal functioning of certain features that make it easier for users to access content.
